About Me

Cinematographer based in Delhi NCR.

SATISH_CINEVISION DOP • DELHI NCR

Hello! I am Satish, operating under SATISH_CINEVISION as a freelance cinematographer, photographer, and video editor based in Chawri Bazaar & Sita Ram Bazar, Delhi, India. My core philosophy revolves around painting with light and shadow to create evocative cinematic moments that resonate emotionally with audiences.

Alongside my creative pursuits, I am currently studying in the second year of my Bachelor of Business Administration (BBA) degree through open learning at IGNOU, while actively preparing for the FTII JET Cinematography entrance examination to deepen my formal expertise in filmmaking at the Film and Television Institute of India.

My professional work spans visual documentary filmmaking, camera operation, studio lighting setups, shot framing, and post-production color grading using industry tools like Adobe Premiere Pro & Lightroom. Whether operating Sony Alpha mirrorless cameras, camcorders, or Insta360 devices, I am dedicated to precision in every frame—from flat S-Log3 raw logs to final Rec.709 theatrical color grades.

Open for collaborations on indie short films, short reels, commercial brand ads, and portraiture across Delhi NCR and Pan-India. Let's create something unforgettable together.
